Hello, and welcome. This is the first post on my new blog — a quiet corner of the internet where I plan to write about software engineering, design, and the craft of building things.

## Why a blog?

In an era dominated by fleeting social media timelines and AI-generated slop, I find myself craving something slower. A blog post is a deliberate act: you sit down, structure your thoughts, and craft something that (hopefully) holds up a year from now.

There is no algorithm here. No engagement metrics. Just words on a page, and you, the reader.

## What to expect

I will write about:

- **Frontend architecture** — patterns, trade-offs, and lessons from real projects
- **Tooling & workflow** — the small optimizations that compound over time
- **Design philosophy** — why certain interfaces *feel* right and others don't
- **Random observations** — because not everything needs to be a tutorial

## A note on design

The blog itself is intentionally minimal. No JavaScript by default. No tracking. No comments (for now). Just clean typography, generous whitespace, and fast load times.

> "Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

That is the spirit of this place.
